# Manual Neuronets

* Manual Neuronets (MNNs) are Green's invention and a way to represent the hidden workings of black-box neural nets. They combine the [Spec to Algorithm](https://github.com/luciangreen/Philosophy/tree/master/s2a), [Starlog](https://github.com/luciangreen/Starlog), Prolog Inductive Debugger, [loop2](https://github.com/luciangreen/loop2), [PLOp](https://github.com/luciangreen/plop)/[AlgebraPLOp](https://github.com/luciangreen/algebraplop), [Detlog](https://github.com/luciangreen/detlog), and [Piglog2](https://github.com/luciangreen/piglog2) repositories. Later, a chatbot incorporating natural language, manual attention, manual time series and other aspects of NNs such as signposting, ranking, analysis, conversations and personality overlays will be added.
