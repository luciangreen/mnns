# Manual Neuronets

* Manual Neuronets (MNNs) are Green's invention and a way to represent the hidden workings of black-box neural nets. They combine the [https://github.com/luciangreen/Philosophy/tree/master/s2a Spec to Algorithm], [https://github.com/luciangreen/Starlog Starlog], [[Prolog Inductive Debugger]], [https://github.com/luciangreen/loop2 loop2], [https://github.com/luciangreen/plop PLOp]/[https://github.com/luciangreen/algebraplop AlgebraPLOp], [https://github.com/luciangreen/detlog Detlog], and [https://github.com/luciangreen/piglog2 Piglog2] repositories. Later, a chatbot incorporating natural language, manual attention, manual time series and other aspects of NNs such as signposting, ranking, analysis, conversations and personality overlays will be added.
